## Recommended offer — proposal for launch

**Website Speed Rescue**, a fixed-scope service for owners of small HTML/CSS/JavaScript business websites.

Proposed pilot price: **US$49**, or a separately agreed local-currency quote. This is a price hypothesis, not a researched market average or a revenue forecast.

Deliverables: baseline measurement for one public page, up to three agreed frontend performance fixes, before/after measurements under comparable conditions, source-code patch, and a short handover. Candidate fixes include image sizing/compression, unnecessary font loading, and render-blocking asset delivery. Changes require authorized source access. A 48-hour delivery window starts only after scope and access are agreed.

Exclude backend rebuilds, checkout changes, paid hosting upgrades, CMS-specific work outside verified capability, guaranteed Lighthouse scores, guaranteed search rankings, or guaranteed sales uplift. If a page has no viable fix in scope, decline the job before taking payment.

### Why this first

Inference: a manually delivered, narrowly scoped service needs fewer prerequisites than launching a subscription app or building ad traffic within seven days. Demand for this specific offer is still unvalidated.

Alternatives considered:
- Paid downloadable frontend template: reusable, but needs distribution and differentiation.
- Subscription performance monitor: recurring potential, but adds billing, hosting, support, and customer acquisition before first revenue.

Market evidence checked on 2026-09-14:
- Fiverr has a website performance services category: https://www.fiverr.com/categories/programming-tech/website-maintenance/performance
- Google's web.dev describes the business relevance of web performance: https://web.dev/learn/performance/why-speed-matters
These establish a service category and a relevant problem, not expected sales or guaranteed outcomes.
